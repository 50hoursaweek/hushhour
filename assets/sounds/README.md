# Typing sounds

Both sets are cut from **Keyboard Soundpack #1 (typing and single keystrokes)** by
**unicaegames**, published on OpenGameArt under **CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication)**:
https://opengameart.org/content/keyboard-soundpack-1-typing-and-single-keystrokes

- `soft/`: six of the quietest presses (020, 009, 005, 016, 012, 003), low-passed at
  2.6 kHz, cut to the key-down stroke and level-matched to -14 dBFS peak.
- `keyboard/`: eight presses (001, 004, 007, 010, 013, 017, 022, 027) left unfiltered,
  cut to the key-down stroke and level-matched to -14 dBFS peak.
